Core Beliefs

Personal Learning Philosophy

As a beginner, I believe genuine mastery comes from patience and consistent effort rather than shortcuts:

  • Step-by-Step Progress: Grasping each concept thoroughly before jumping ahead to complex abstractions.

  • Improving Through Experience: Viewing mistakes as valuable feedback that sharpens intuition and analytical precision.
  • Honest Reflection: Continuously assessing what I know and actively seeking guidance on what I need to improve.
